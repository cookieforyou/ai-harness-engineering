# E2E Delivery Harness - AI Engineering Asset Library

## Project Overview

E2E Delivery Harness is a comprehensive AI engineering asset library designed for the full software delivery lifecycle, providing standardized and reusable AI workflow templates.

## Design Philosophy

This asset library follows a modular design approach of **"Role + Skill + Scenario"**:

- **Agent**: Defines AI identity, working rules, and available tools
- **Skill**: Encapsulates domain-specific capabilities and procedures
- **Instruction**: Provides operational guidelines and quality checkpoints
- **Prompt**: Guides AI to generate compliant output
- **Scenario**: Combines multiple assets into complete business workflows

## Key Benefits

1. **Full Lifecycle Coverage**: Covers six major phases from requirement analysis to monitoring operations
2. **Standardized Output**: Clear input/output specifications and quality checkpoints for each phase
3. **Role-based Collaboration**: Defined AI role responsibilities, supporting pipeline-style collaboration
4. **Extensible Architecture**: Supports customization and flexible composition

## Applicable Scenarios

- Full lifecycle planning for new projects
- AI-assisted development for existing projects
- Standardization of team collaboration processes
